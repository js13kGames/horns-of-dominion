The Rainbow Lands has kept no single crown since the Accord broke. It's twenty largest cities are divided into five realms: **Crimson Mane**, **Sunmane Reach**, **Verdant Glade**, **Azure Spire**, **Violet Veil**. And every one of them keeps a horn with a single note in it: *all of it, or nothing*.

You are the king of one of these realms, living on an age where total conquest and unification is the only solution.

## Three ages to fight in

- **I. Rise of the Golden Horn** · Hearth 23rd, 13312 — a close-packed kingdom where every realm is a neighbour.
- **II. The Broken Accord** · Lumin 10th, 13986 — long and thin, and it comes apart at two cities.
- **III. The Radiant Spire** · Verdant 13th, 14001 — more roads than any age before, and longer.

Time runs in the Mazurian Age: thirteen months of twenty-eight days, **Auriel** through **Lunaris**. Your campaign is scored in the days it took you.

## Playing

- **Click your city** → raise a warband. It costs 💎 gold and 👥 populace, and takes time.
- **Click your warband** → it marches at your word (the cursor turns to a crosshair).
- **Click a city** → go there, however many roads away.
- **Click anywhere else** → stand down. `space` pauses, `1`–`4` set the pace.

## What every commander learns

🔱 Footmen are raised anywhere. 🦄 Unicorns and 🐘 behemoths are bred, and only two cities in a realm breed them; take those cities and the pens come with them. Unicorns ambush columns on the road, while behemoths bring walls down, and in open field footmen break behemoths.

**Warriors tire.** Marching wearies a host; fighting wearies it four times faster. Winded, it moves at half pace, which usually means it cannot get away.

**A city remembers the banner it was drafted under.** Conquer it and it seethes, riots, and throws you out unless you leave men enough to sit on it, and those are men not marching on the next wall.

**The fog hides intent, never ground.** You always see the roads; what walks them is another matter.
